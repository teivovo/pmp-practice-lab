# PMP question-bank check

Checked 28 September 2026. This is a review of public provider information, not an audit of every paid question or a certification of exam-equivalent difficulty.

The [PMI July 2026 exam page](https://www.pmi.org/certifications/project-management-pmp/new-exam) is the reference for currency: the ECO defines the syllabus, with People 33%, Process 41%, and Business Environment 26%. A PMBOK edition label alone does not establish full coverage.

## How this informs the local bank

The app contains 400 original educational questions and explanations. External resources informed the review of scenario-based reasoning, format variety, and current topics; their questions, answer choices, explanations, and exhibits were not imported or closely paraphrased. No permission to republish paid content is assumed. Free access does not by itself establish redistribution rights; no external-content licence is relied upon for this release.

The expanded bank includes stakeholder and conflict scenarios, predictive/adaptive tailoring, numerical cost and schedule reasoning, linked cases, multiple responses, matching, pull-down lists, original charts, and clickable diagrams. AI, sustainability, value, governance, adoption, and external change are included. Independent trainer review and psychometric calibration remain outstanding; practice scores are not pass predictions.
